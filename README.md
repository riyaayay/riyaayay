## currently

I'm a software engineering intern building full-stack systems, while most of my side quests somehow turn into AI projects.

I like working where **models meet actual systems** — RAG, retrieval, agents, computer vision, forecasting, control, and the slightly annoying engineering required to make all of it behave outside a notebook.

Lately I've been especially interested in systems that can **question their own answers**, handle uncertainty, and know when *not* to pretend they're right.

Before my current internship, I worked on AI/Deep Learning at a government DAE facility. I was also one of **3,000 students selected for Amazon ML Summer School from 134,000+ applicants**.

## things I've built

Not everything I've built fits neatly into one category, which is probably a good thing.

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

### [VoltAgent](https://github.com/riyaayay/voltagent)

A RAG system with a built-in **"wait, are we sure?"** step.

Hybrid retrieval + a hallucination-verifier agent. Improved Recall@6 by **20 points**, while the verifier caught **8/10** deliberately wrong answers in testing.

`Python` `LangGraph` `Elasticsearch` `Qdrant` `FastAPI`

</td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

### [Gridlock 2.0](https://github.com/riyaayay/gridlock-2-0)

Traffic forecasting for the **Flipkart GRID Challenge**.

Combined tree ensembles with a PyTorch TCN and trained on an NVIDIA A100 through GCP across **77K+ records** and **1,249 regions**.

`PyTorch` `LightGBM` `XGBoost` `CatBoost` `GCP`

</td>
  </tr>

  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

### [Autonomous Production Choke Controller](https://github.com/riyaayay/autonomous-production-choke-controller)

An uncertainty-aware Model Predictive Control system built for the **Honeywell Campus Hackathon**.

Data-driven constraint back-off took safety violations from **42/70 → 0/196** in testing.

`Python` `NumPy` `pandas`

</td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

### [PolarSolarNet](https://github.com/riyaayay/polarsolarnet)

A coordinate-aware CNN for **HALO CME detection**, submitted to ACM Multimedia 2026.

Reached **0.948 AUC** across 27 years of solar data, with a low false-alarm rate for the task.

`PyTorch` `CatBoost`

</td>
  </tr>

  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

### [NYAYA](https://github.com/riyaayay/nyaya)

An AI compliance and bias-auditing platform working across **1M+ records**.

Uses Pearson correlation, VIF and SHAP analysis, backed by role-differentiated microservices.

`FastAPI` `React` `Firestore` `BigQuery` `SHAP`

</td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

### [TaskFlow](https://github.com/riyaayay/taskflow)

A production-style REST API for task management.

Built with a focus on clean backend architecture and **90%+ unit-test coverage**.

`Java` `Spring Boot` `MySQL` `JUnit`

</td>
