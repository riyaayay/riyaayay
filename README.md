### What I actually do

Most of my time goes into applied AI, retrieval architectures, and figuring out why distributed pipelines break at 2 AM. 

I care less about getting an LLM to generate convincing prose and more about building the deterministic harnesses around it—verifier agents, constraint enforcement, and systems that fail gracefully instead of confidently hallucinating.

* **Engineering:** Full-Stack SWE Intern by day; building autonomous agents & deep learning pipelines by night.
* **Past runs:** Deep learning at an Indian Department of Atomic Energy (DAE) facility; picked for Amazon ML Summer School (top ~2% of 134k+ applicants).
