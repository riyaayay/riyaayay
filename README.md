## a little about me

I'm a software engineering intern who keeps finding excuses to build AI systems.

I work across **AI/ML, backend systems, retrieval, agents, computer vision, forecasting, and control** — basically wherever a model needs to leave the notebook and become an actual system.

Recently I've been especially interested in systems that can **check themselves, handle uncertainty, and know when they might be wrong**.

Before my current internship, I worked on AI/Deep Learning at a government DAE facility. I was also one of **3,000 students selected for Amazon ML Summer School from 134,000+ applicants**.
