## About

I build AI systems and the infrastructure underneath them — mostly RAG pipelines, retrieval, and the occasional control system that has to stay safe under uncertainty. A lot of my work is less about getting a model to answer and more about getting it to know when it shouldn't: verifier agents, uncertainty-aware controllers, bias audits.

I'm currently a Full-Stack Software Engineering Intern. Before that, I worked on an AI/Deep Learning internship at a government DAE facility, and I was one of 3,000 people selected for Amazon's ML Summer School out of 134,000+ applicants.
